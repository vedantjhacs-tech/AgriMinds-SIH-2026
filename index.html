Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about booking procurement slots.

Is AgriMinds an official government portal? ▼

No, AgriMinds is a prototype built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 by Team AgriMinds (Maharaja Agrasen University). All data presented here is for simulation and demonstration purposes.

How do I book a procurement slot? ▼

Simply navigate to the 'Book Slot' page, select your Mandi, crop type, quantity, and preferred date and time. An instant QR token will be generated for you.

What happens if I miss my time slot? ▼

You can view and manage your booked slots under 'My Bookings'. If needed, you can cancel an upcoming slot and rebook for a later date or time slot.
