About AgriMinds Portal

AgriMinds is an advanced digital agricultural procurement and queue management portal designed to revolutionize how farmers interact with mandis. Our goal is to eliminate long waiting hours, bring complete transparency to MSP payouts, and streamline the entire process from crop harvesting to direct bank transfers.

🌱 Our Mission

To digitize procurement operations, ensure zero waiting hours at mandis, and bring 100% financial transparency to farmers.

💡 Core Innovation

Real-time token queue tracking, secure OTP authentication, and automated MSP payout calculations backed by robust technology.
