About AgriMinds

AgriMinds is a Smart India Hackathon 2026 prototype designed to address one of Indian agriculture's most pressing logistical challenges: excessive mandi wait times and opaque procurement processes.

The Problem

Farmers routinely wait 6 to 12 hours in physical queues at agricultural procurement centers without real-time updates, clear price guarantees, or digitised gate entries.

Our Solution

A lightweight, 3-pillar digital platform combining Digital Slot Booking, Real-Time Queue Tracking, and 100% Verified MSP Transparency.
